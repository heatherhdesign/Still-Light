Resources

Still-Light is designed as a space for presence, reflection, and gentle connection—but it is not a substitute for professional care. If you are looking for support, you deserve access to people and services trained to help. The resources below are available if you want to talk, need assistance, or are seeking next steps.

If you are in immediate danger, please call 911 (U.S.) or your local emergency number. You can also reach out to:

Immediate Support
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (U.S.)
Call or text 988
Chat: https://988lifeline.org
Available 24/7, free and confidential support

Crisis Text Line
Text HOME to 741741 (U.S. & Canada)
https://www.crisistextline.org
Connect with a trained counselor via text

Sexual Assault & Trauma Support
RAINN (Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network)
https://www.rainn.org
Hotline: 800-656-HOPE (4673)
Live chat available online
Confidential support, information, and pathways to care

National Sexual Violence Resource Center (NSVRC)
https://www.nsvrc.org
Education, resources, and support tools

Domestic Violence Support
National Domestic Violence Hotline
https://www.thehotline.org
Call: 1-800-799-7233
Text: START to 88788
Live chat available
Support for anyone experiencing relationship abuse

Additional Support
SAMHSA National Helpline
https://www.samhsa.gov/find-help/national-helpline
Call: 1-800-662-HELP (4357)
Treatment referral and information service (mental health & substance use)

You don’t have to move quickly. You don’t have to decide anything all at once. If any of these feel like a possible next step, they’re here for you, and if not, that’s okay too.
