Approach

Still-Light is shaped by a trauma-informed approach to digital design.

Rather than encouraging interaction, the environment is intentionally guided by a “less is more” philosophy. Each space is designed to reduce demand, allowing the user to engage—or not—on their own terms.

Choice is central.
There are no required paths, no timed interactions, no gamification, and no expectation to move forward. Navigation remains open and flexible, offering a sense of control without pressure.

Nothing is asked to be given.
There is no need to share, respond, or explain. The environment does not assume readiness, nor does it attempt to guide the user toward a specific outcome.

Presence is enough.
Whether someone stays briefly or lingers, engages or remains still, the experience holds space without judgment or requirement.

In a digital landscape that often asks for attention, input, and reaction, Still-Light is designed to do the opposite. It creates room for quiet, for pause, and for moments that are self-directed and unobserved.
